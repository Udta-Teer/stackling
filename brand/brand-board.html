The name

// recommendation + alternates — your call
RECOMMENDED
Truss
The triangulated frame that lets a bridge or roof carry huge load without buckling. One syllable, structural-engineering native, and it literally means "the thing that holds at scale." The mark doubles as a distributed-node graph.
collision: low · verb-adjacent · app-store clean
Keystone
The wedge stone that locks an arch together — the core skill everything rests on. Premium and confident.
collision: medium (KeystoneJS, habit books)
Quorum
The majority that must agree for a distributed system to commit. Insider term, sounds smart, great for interview positioning.
collision: medium (blockchain, gov-tech)
Shard
A native system-design term (splitting data to scale). Short, sharp, techy, memorable.
collision: medium-high (gaming, crypto)

Why a beaver // not an owl

The only animal that reshapes its environment by building. Beavers engineer dams — literal systems that manage flow and load. Industrious, calm, a little clever. It owns a lane Duolingo's owl can't.

Personality

Encouraging but honest. Rivet doesn't just clap — it points at the bottleneck. "Nice — but what happens when this shard gets hot?" It embodies the push-harder depth rule.

How it shows up

Holds the amber Signal as a streak flame. Hard hat for boss/capstone lessons. Reacts on wrong answers (thoughtful, never mocking). Celebrates a completed unit by adding a beam to its dam.
