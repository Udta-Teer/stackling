The name

// Stackling — locked

Stackling = stack (a word every beginner meets via Stack Overflow, and literally what system design is — layers stacked together) + -ling (a small, young thing: duckling, fledgling — the learner you are at the start, and a nod to Duolingo). It is on-theme for the subject, friendly to a noob, and — unlike Byte, Chip, Stack, Nibble, Kernel, and a dozen twists we checked — actually ownable: no app, no software trademark conflict.

The Stackling
// the top block, come to life

Why this beats the beaver // computer-native

The mascot is the logo: the amber top block with two eyes — a small stacked-block creature. Unmistakably digital, born from the brand itself, and it literally grows a taller stack as you complete units. No unrelated animal required.

Personality

Encouraging but honest. The Stackling doesn't just cheer — it points at the bottleneck: "Nice — but what happens when this shard gets hot?" It embodies the push-harder depth rule.

Still on the table

If you'd rather have a face-forward character, the engineer-bot, chip, or computer-mouse ideas still stand and can pair with the name. Say which direction and I'll develop it.
